ClipGaff — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

ClipGaff is a private tool used by the ClipGaff team to upload clips to the team's own TikTok account.

  1. Data we access. With your permission, the tool reads basic profile info (display name, avatar) of the connected TikTok account and uploads videos to it. That is all.
  2. Data we store. The TikTok access token is stored on the team's own computer so we do not have to log in every time. Nothing is stored on a server.
  3. Data we share. None. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  4. Data from other people. The tool does not collect data about viewers, followers or any other TikTok user.
  5. Deleting your data. Disconnect the app in TikTok settings, or email clipgaff0@gmail.com, and the stored token is deleted.
  6. Contact. clipgaff0@gmail.com
