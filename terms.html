ClipGaff — Terms of Service

Last updated: 25 September 2026

ClipGaff is a private tool used by the ClipGaff team to upload short, credited livestream highlight clips to the team's own TikTok account.

  1. Who can use it. Only the ClipGaff team. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.
  2. What it does. It sends video files and captions from our computer to our own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.
  3. Content. Every clip credits the original streamer. Streamers can request a takedown at any time by emailing clipgaff0@gmail.com and it is removed the same day.
  4. TikTok's rules. Use of the tool is also subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  5. No warranty. The tool is provided as is, with no guarantees.
  6. Contact. clipgaff0@gmail.com
